# Publication Policy

This is a public reference library, not an archive of source material.

- Prefer canonical links, short quotations, and original summaries.
- Attribute factual claims to the sources recorded in each concept's `sources` frontmatter.
- Treat agent-written summaries as unverified until a `verified` event records a human or deterministic check.
- Do not publish credentials, private notes, personal data, confidential work material, or content whose redistribution is not permitted.
- Knowledge summaries in `docs/` remain `status: draft` until a human review promotes them.
- Correct material errors in place when practical and record a short note in `log.md`.
- Preserve deprecated concepts for link stability and explain what supersedes them.
